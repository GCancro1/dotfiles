- **Category:** tax
- **Status:** 🔴 active
- **Priority:** high

- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-05

**Context:**
 Only consider for brokerage and options accounts. Need to evaluate which positions are below cost basis and whether swapping to similar (but not substantially identical) ETFs makes sense. Consider wash sale rules — if buying back the same or substantially identical security within 30 days, the loss is disallowed. In a Roth IRA, harvested losses don't provide a tax deduction (since Roth gains are tax-free), so the calculus is different than in a taxable account. The main benefit would be resetting cost basis higher for future distributions, or freeing up capital to reposition.
